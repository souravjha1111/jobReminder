CareerClock — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2025

CareerClock is a Chrome extension designed to help job seekers stay consistent by sending scheduled reminders and opening saved job links. This Privacy Policy explains what (if any) data CareerClock collects, how that data is used, and the choices you have.

Summary

CareerClock does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information to external servers. All user-created data (reminders, links, and settings) is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's chrome.storage.sync. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.

What data the extension stores

  • Reminders: Names, schedule type (daily or interval), time or interval settings, paused state.
  • Saved links: Link name and URL (up to 5 links per reminder).
  • Settings: Notification preferences, sound on/off, auto-open preferences.

Where data is stored

All data is stored locally using Chrome's chrome.storage.sync. This storage syncs across Chrome browsers where you are signed in with the same Google account, but the data is still handled by Chrome and not transmitted to any of the developer's servers.

No analytics or tracking

CareerClock does not include analytics, telemetry, or third-party trackers. The extension does not use cookies, crash-reporting services, or advertising networks.

Data sharing and third parties

CareerClock does not share user data with third parties. There are no backend servers operated by the developer and no integrations that transmit your reminders or links outside your browser.

Data retention and removal

Your data remains in Chrome storage until you delete it or uninstall the extension. If you uninstall CareerClock, Chrome will remove the extension's local data in accordance with Chrome's storage behavior.

Permissions

CareerClock requests the following Chrome permissions, each of which is required for core functionality:

  • storage — to save reminders, links, and settings locally.
  • alarms — to schedule reminders at the requested times or intervals.
  • notifications — to display desktop notifications when a reminder fires.
  • tabs — to open saved job links in new tabs when you click "Open All".

Why these permissions are needed

The requested permissions are limited and used only to implement CareerClock's single purpose: helping you stay consistent in your job search by scheduling reminders and opening links. None of the permissioned APIs are used to transmit your data outside the browser.

User choices

  • You may disable notifications in Chrome or in the extension settings (sound, auto-open options).
  • You may remove or edit any saved reminder or link at any time from the extension UI.
  • To remove all stored data, uninstall the extension; Chrome will clear extension storage.

Note: This policy describes the current behavior of CareerClock. If the extension's architecture changes in the future (for example, adding a server component), this policy will be updated and users will be notified by publishing a new version.
